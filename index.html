Memora

Memora is a mobile rhythm game I've been making since late 2024, and I handle the programming, art, music direction and storyboarding myself. The lines you play on move and spin with the music and can even freeze or run backwards, notes can fly at one another or at points that are still moving, and a visual-novel story about memories and photography runs alongside the songs. I also built the charting editor inside Unity that I use to make every level.

In the write-up I go through how the game keeps in time with the music, how its four kinds of note are judged, how a note can aim at something that is still moving, and how a whole field of notes can freeze or rewind mid-song. It also covers the charting tools, including a motion library built by analysing several hundred published charts, the storyboard pipeline behind the story mode, and how I tracked down a slowdown in the editor that took each repaint from around 120 ms to 15 ms.
