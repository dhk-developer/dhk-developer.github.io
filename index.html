What I'm working on now

Memora

Memora is the project that takes up most of my evenings and weekends. It's a rhythm game in which the lines you tap on move around the screen, notes can drift through open space or fly at one another, and nearly everything on screen is choreographed to the music. Making charts for it by hand quickly became impractical, so I also built an editor inside Unity that lets me jump to any point in a song and see exactly what the player will see.

In the write-up I go through how the game keeps in time with the music, how a note can aim at something that is still moving, and how I tracked down a slowdown in the editor that took each repaint from around 120 ms to 15 ms. There's also a small timing game on that page you can try in your browser.
