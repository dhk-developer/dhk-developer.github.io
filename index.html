Main project · 2025 to now

Memora

My biggest project. Notes ride on moving lines, float free or fly at other notes, and the whole screen is choreographed to the song. I also built the editor I use to write the charts, because I needed to preview any moment of a song without playing it from the start.

The write-up covers how the timing works, how notes aim at things that are still moving, and how I found and fixed a slowdown in the editor (repaints went from about 120 ms to 15 ms). There's also a small timing demo you can play in the browser.
