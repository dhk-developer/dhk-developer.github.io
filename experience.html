Experience

So far my jobs have all been on the analysis and consulting side, and the list below is what I did in each of them. I've left out client names and anything else confidential, so it's more about the kind of work than about who it was for.

Infotechtion

Business Analyst (Change)

Oct 2024 – present

A consultancy that runs Microsoft 365 data-governance, records-management and information-protection programmes for large organisations.

  • Write business and technical requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria in Azure DevOps, often starting from goals that the client hasn't fully pinned down yet, so a lot of the job is asking the questions that turn a broad aim into something testable.
  • Plan and run research sessions with the people who manage records and information governance day to day, and work out from them which needs and process problems should be tackled first.
  • Interview business owners and engineers during discovery, and make sure both groups come away with the same understanding of what is being built.
  • Map how processes work at the moment and how they should work afterwards, including the steps where data is brought in and checked.
  • Help people get used to new ways of working through training sessions, communications and follow-up once a change has gone live.

Xander Talent

Technical Consultant

Dec 2023 – 2024

A technical consultancy that took on data and CRM work for its clients.

  • Looked into why a client was making so little use of their Salesforce CRM, comparing how they worked at the time with how they could work, and recommended what would need to change.
  • Redesigned an ETL process so that it no longer collected duplicate form fields or extracted data nobody used, and presented the results to the client.
  • Put together short reports from the client's data that people without a technical background could act on.
  • Worked in Scrum sprints with regular reviews alongside the client.

Jaffe & Co

US/UK Tax Trainee

Jul – Nov 2023

A tax practice working with clients who have obligations in both the US and the UK.

  • Prepared and checked US and UK tax returns for individuals and companies, which meant working to two sets of rules at once.
  • Reviewed client documents to make sure they were complete and valid before anything was filed.

University of Cambridge · In2Research

Computational Research Assistant

Jul – Sep 2022

A three-month summer research placement in computational materials science.

  • Wrote a Python package that generates random polyethylene structures and relaxes them with a force field, following the AIRSS structure-search method.
  • Presented the results as a poster at the In2Research summer conference.

Imperial College London

MSci Chemistry (2:1)

2019 – 2023

My dissertation looked at how reproducible it is to build Gaussian16 inputs for large biological assemblies, and I developed a workflow to make the process less error-prone.
