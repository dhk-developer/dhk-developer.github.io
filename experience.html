Experience

My roles so far have been in consultancy and research, and each entry below describes what the job involved day to day. The skills at the bottom of the page are each paired with the project or role where I used them.

Infotechtion

Business Analyst / Change Management Consultant

Oct 2024 – present

A consultancy for Microsoft 365 data governance, records management and information protection.

  • Build Power BI dashboards that track classification adoption and compliance across more than 10,000 users, so that leadership can see risk exposure and adoption trends.
  • Query Microsoft 365 audit logs and Purview data with SQL and Python to analyse user behaviour, sharing patterns and how well policies are working.
  • Automated the weekly reporting workflow in Python, which cut the time spent on manual reporting by about 70%.
  • Map how data is ingested, validated and handled when something goes wrong, and define controls such as record counts, checksums, audit logging and reconciliation so that data issues stay traceable and testable.
  • Write requirements, data definitions, user stories and acceptance criteria in Azure DevOps, and interview business owners and engineers until both groups agree on one view of the solution.
  • Support a global Microsoft 365 data-protection rollout reaching more than 10,000 end users, including training and follow-up.

Xander Talent

Technical Consultant (placed at Grant Thornton UK)

Dec 2023 – Oct 2024

A technical consultancy. Most of my time there was a client placement with Grant Thornton UK's R&D tax team.

  • Interviewed technical teams at more than 100 client companies and wrote the technical narratives for their HMRC R&D tax relief claims, covering software, data and engineering projects.
  • Reconciled project cost datasets in SQL and Excel to find anomalies before submission.
  • Turned complex technical detail into clear, evidence-based reports for tax specialists, written to HMRC's criteria for qualifying R&D.
  • Redesigned a client's ETL process so that it no longer collected duplicate form fields or extracted data nobody used, and presented the impact to the client.
  • Looked into why a client was making so little use of their Salesforce CRM, compared how they worked with how they could work, and recommended changes, delivered in Scrum sprints with regular client reviews.
